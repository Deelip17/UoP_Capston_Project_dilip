# Demo Web Shop requirements (synthetic)

## Accounts
- The system shall allow a registered customer to log in with an email address and password.
- The system shall lock an account for 15 minutes after 5 failed login attempts.

## Catalogue
- A visitor can search the product catalogue by keyword and see matching products.
- The product page should load quickly.

## Checkout
As a customer, I want to pay for my cart by credit card so that I can complete my order.

As a customer, I want an easy checkout.

- The system must always send an order confirmation and never send an order confirmation for cancelled orders.
